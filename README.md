# 🍞 Food & Beverages Sales Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive Power BI dashboard that analyzes food and beverage sales performance across products, salespeople, sales channels, and time (2019–2021).

![Dashboard Preview](images/dashboard.png)

## 📌 Business Objective

Give management a single view of sales performance to answer:

- How much revenue and how many orders are we generating?
- Which product groups drive the most revenue?
- Who are the top-performing salespeople?
- Which sales channels contribute the most?
- How do sales change by month and quarter?

## 📊 Key Metrics

| KPI | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Revenue | 17.91M |
| Total Orders | 53K |
| ATP (Average Transaction Price) | 340.73 |
| Total Items Sold | 6M |

## 🔍 Key Insights

- **Wheat Flour is the top product group** at 4.5M revenue, followed by Oil (2.9M) and Yeasts (2.2M).
- **Food dominates sales**, making up 90.21% of orders, while Drinks account for 9.79%.
- **Retail is the biggest channel** (48.56%), followed by Distributors (34.05%) and Online (17.39%).
- **Carla Ferreira is the top salesperson** with 4.7M in revenue, well ahead of the rest of the team.
- **Sales are stronger in the second half of the year.** Q3 and Q4 together bring in about 10.7M of the 17.9M total, and revenue peaks around September–October.
- **February is the weakest month** for revenue.

## 💡 Recommendations

- Protect and expand the top product groups (Wheat Flour, Oil, Yeasts) with stock planning ahead of Q3/Q4.
- Grow the Online channel, which is the smallest share at 17.39%.
- Run promotions in Q1 and Q2 to reduce the seasonal dip.
- Look into what the top salesperson does differently and share it with the team.
- Grow the drinks category, which is under 10% of orders.

## 🛠️ Tools & Techniques

- **Power BI Desktop**
- **Data modeling:** Sales, Product, and Date tables
- **DAX measures:** Total Revenue, Total Orders, ATP, Total Items Sold
- **Visuals:** KPI cards, bar, column and line combo charts, donut charts, year slicer
